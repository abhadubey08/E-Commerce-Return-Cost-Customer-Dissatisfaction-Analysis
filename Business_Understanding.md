# Business Understanding

## Problem Statement

E-commerce companies face significant challenges due to product returns. High return volumes increase operational costs, reduce profitability, negatively affect customer satisfaction, and contribute to environmental waste.

Understanding the factors driving returns can help businesses reduce costs, improve customer experience, and make better operational decisions.

## Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

* Analyze return patterns across different product categories.
* Identify the major reasons behind product returns.
* Measure the financial impact of returns on revenue, profit, and return costs.
* Understand customer dissatisfaction trends based on demographics and locations.
* Evaluate the effect of discount strategies on returns and profitability.
* Assess sustainability impact through CO₂ emissions and packaging waste.
* Generate actionable recommendations to reduce returns and improve business performance.

## Key Business Questions

1. Which product categories generate the highest number of returns?
2. What are the most common reasons for product returns?
3. How do returns affect profitability and return costs?
4. Which customer segments are associated with higher return activity?
5. What is the relationship between discount levels and return behavior?
6. Which categories contribute most to packaging waste and CO₂ emissions?
7. What actions can be taken to reduce customer dissatisfaction and return-related losses?

## Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it:

* Identifies the major drivers of product returns.
* Quantifies the financial and sustainability impact of returns.
* Provides meaningful insights through data analysis and dashboards.
* Delivers practical business recommendations supported by data.
