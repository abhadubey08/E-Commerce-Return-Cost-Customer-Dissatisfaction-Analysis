# Business Understanding

## Project Title

**E-Commerce Return Cost Optimization & Customer Experience Analytics**

---

# 1. Introduction

Product returns are one of the most significant hidden costs in the e-commerce industry. Every returned order results in additional logistics expenses, operational inefficiencies, inventory handling costs, customer dissatisfaction, and environmental impact.

This project aims to analyze customer return behavior and identify the key factors contributing to return-related costs and customer dissatisfaction. The insights generated from this analysis will help management make data-driven decisions to improve profitability, enhance customer experience, and reduce operational waste.

---

# 2. Business Problem Statement

The e-commerce company is experiencing increasing operational costs due to product returns. Management lacks visibility into the factors driving returns, customer dissatisfaction, and profitability loss.

The company needs to understand:

* Why customers are returning products.
* Which customer segments contribute most to return-related losses.
* Whether discount strategies are increasing return rates.
* How shipping and payment methods influence return behavior.
* Which locations generate the highest return costs.
* What environmental impact is caused by returned orders.

Without understanding these factors, the company cannot effectively reduce return-related expenses or improve customer experience.
